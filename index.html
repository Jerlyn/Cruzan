Crucian is the Virgin Islands Creole dialect of St. Croix — an English-based creole born from enslaved West Africans, Danish colonial rule, and centuries of Caribbean contact. Explore its dictionary, grammar, and proverbs two ways.

The Web App

The full archive: 216 dictionary terms, 78 proverbs, grammar notes, and the heritage story behind the language. Works offline once installed.

  • Searchable dictionary & glossary
  • Proverbs, grammar, and heritage timeline
  • Installable, works offline, light + dark themes
Open the Archive →

To install: open the app, then use the Install button in its header (desktop/Android), or Safari's Add to Home Screen (iOS).

The Extension

A companion for Chrome and Firefox: word of the day, quick lookup, and right-click search — the dictionary a click away without leaving your page.

  • Word of the day on every new tab
  • Right-click any word to look it up
  • Same dictionary data as the web app
